# Music credits

The Space Odyssey Simulator includes an original Web Audio ambient score and an optional **80s Retro** playlist. The playlist uses only tracks with a license that allows redistribution in a public web app (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 or CC0). No copyrighted commercial recordings are included.

Tracks are bundled under `public/music/` as 128 kbps MP3 files.

## License notes

Kevin MacLeod’s incompetech catalog is offered under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, as stated on the official [Music FAQ](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html) and on each track’s details page (attribution block: “Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License”). The standard credit format is:

```
Title Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
```

The same credits appear in the app’s **About** panel and in the Music panel while the 80s Retro playlist is selected.
